I am a PhD student in the Computer Systems Group at IIIT Hyderabad and a Computer Systems Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services. My research is on genome sequence alignment using spatial accelerators, in particular the AMD Versal ACAP AI Engine array.

Before moving into systems research I spent about 11 years in industry across Java and J2EE development, machine learning, deep learning and data visualization. At TCS I work on heterogeneous computing for AMD platforms: Ryzen AI bug triaging, roofline analysis support for Radeon GPUs, runtime scheduling of workloads across integrated CPU, GPU and NPU, and offloading workloads to Ryzen NPUs. I also explore memory-centric computing, including processing-in-memory, processing-using-memory and non-volatile memory.

Outside work I enjoy football, swimming and novels, and I am a Real Madrid fan.
